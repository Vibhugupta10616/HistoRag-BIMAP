# H2 — Patient Correlation on a Patch Level

## Hypothesis

How do patients relate to each other based on their patch-level embeddings?

Two questions answered at patient level, using one encoder's pre-computed embeddings:

---

## Q1 — Do aggregated patient embeddings cluster by anatomical site?

**Method:** Embed all patches → mean pool per patient → 1 vector/patient → UMAP +
patient-patient correlation matrix.

**Expected UMAP:** 3 distinct clusters — Oropharynx (OPX), Larynx (LAR), Oral Cavity (OC).

**Expected heatmap:** Block-diagonal pattern — high similarity within each site group,
lower similarity across groups.

**Why:** WSI-level aggregated embeddings capture anatomical location; patients from the
same primary site have more similar tissue composition.

---

## Q2 — How similar is cancer tissue across patients regardless of site?

**Method:** Filter to tumour patches only (from `.geojson` annotations) → UMAP on
individual patches + patient-patient correlation matrix (adaptive aggregation).

**Expected UMAP:** Patches from all three sites are **mixed** — no site-based separation.

**Expected heatmap:** Uniformly high similarity across all patients — cancer tissue shares
common histopathological patterns regardless of anatomical site.

### Adaptive aggregation (Q2 correlation matrix)

Check median tumour patch count per patient after `.geojson` extraction:

| Median tumour patches/patient | Correlation matrix method |
|---|---|
| ≥ 20 | Aggregate: mean pool → 1 vector/patient → patient × patient matrix |
| < 20 | No aggregation: patch × patch matrix, axes grouped by patient |

See `histoRAG/correlate.py → decide_aggregation`.
